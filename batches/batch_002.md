## 3. Most Interesting and Noteworthy Pages

1. **Peer-reviewed papers next to 9/11 "truther" material.** The site hosts real journal publications, such as *Il Nuovo Cimento* 1994 (http://www.jp-petit.org/science/f300/f3100/f3102.htm) and *Astrophysics and Space Science* 1995 (http://www.jp-petit.org/science/f300/f3200/f3201bis.htm). The same site also runs the Pentagon "no plane" dossier (http(s)://www.jp-petit.org/Divers/PENTAGATE/temoignage_CNN.htm). This contrast between credentialed science and conspiracy content defines the site.

2. **A genuine contribution to mathematics.** In 1981, Petit and Jérôme Souriau published an analytic representation of Boy's surface in the *Comptes Rendus* (https://www.jp-petit.org/maths_f/boy/f5101.htm). The page adds copper-wire models made with a sculptor and disputes over credit.

3. **A topology model passed around at a Lacanian congress.** In 1985, about 400 psychoanalysts handled Petit's crosscap cube (https://www.jp-petit.org/science/maths_f/obj_geom/crosscapcube.html). It is a rare meeting point of topology and psychoanalysis, and Petit treats it with humour.

4. **The "lactoèdre" may be a hoax.** The page's sources are "Grossman 1956" and images by "Iéronimus Holstein and Miroslav Milka" (https://www.jp-petit.org/maths_f/obj_geom/lactoedre.html). Combined with "lacto-" (milk) and Holstein (a dairy cow breed), the names strongly suggest a deadpan joke. Similarly, the story that Möbius named his strip while fastening his trousers (http://www.jp-petit.org/science/maths_f/obj_geom/moebius_un_demi_t.html) looks apocryphal.

5. **The "monoèdre."** Petit claims to have invented a polyhedron with one face, one edge and no vertices (http://www.jp-petit.org/science/maths_f/obj_geom/mono_transp.htm). It is a striking geometric curiosity.

6. **A 1975 proposal for an MHD "aerodyne."** This *Comptes Rendus* note proposes an MHD-propelled aircraft concept (http(s)://www.jp-petit.org/science/mhd/m_mhd_e/CRAS_1975_en.htm). It connects to Sakharov (http://www.jp-petit.org/science/Z-machine/Sakharov_MHD/Sakharov_MHD0.htm) and to Petit's later "Legacy of Sakharov" paper.

7. **A detailed record of a peer-review dispute.** Petit's account of his conflict with *Astronomy & Astrophysics* describes about 60 referee questions and seven manuscript versions (https://www.jp-petit.org/science/gal_port/Affaire_Lequeux.htm). Related pages include the 1992 simulation rejected by "a dozen journals" (https://www.jp-petit.org/Cosmo_astro/animation_1992.htm) and the CNRS evaluation letter (http://www.jp-petit.org/science/chronique_science/section14.htm). Together they are a rare first-person record of a heterodox scientist's grievances.

8. **"Plugstars" as an alternative to black holes (2024).** These recent papers show the Janus program still active decades later (http://www.jp-petit.org/papers/cosmo/HAL2/?C=D;O=D).

9. **A UFO advocate writing skeptically about the paranormal.** Despite Petit's ufology (including the Ummo affair, https://www.jp-petit.org/OVNIS/ummo_sciences_word_preuves.htm), his "Paranormal vs. Artifacts" series looks for ordinary explanations such as illusions, experimental error and fraud (https://www.jp-petit.org/Divers/Paranormal/Paranormal.htm). He also debunks a local UFO sighting (https://www.jp-petit.org/OVNIS/UN_OVNI_A_VINON/ovni_a_vinon.htm).

10. **A deeply personal elegy.** "La Poule Noire" is dedicated to Petit's son, who died in 1990 (http://www.jp-petit.org/AVANT_DE_VOUS_QUITTER/Chansons/La_Poule_Noire.htm). The song uses the refrain "Lanturlu," the name of his comic-book hero. The section title "Avant de vous quitter" ("Before leaving you") gives the creative pages a legacy feel.

11. **An engineering proposal six days after 9/11.** On 17 September 2001, Petit posted an unpatented cable-descent system for evacuating skyscrapers (https://www.jp-petit.org/dangers/danger_eng/tower_evacuation/tower_evacuation.htm). It is a constructive response, unlike his later Pentagon pages.

12. **Raw labour-history documents.** Transcripts of the Gardanne coal mine's safety committee in 2003 (e.g., https://www.jp-petit.org/Gardanne/rapport09.htm) are unusual primary sources. They connect to the claim of secret nuclear tests in a Gardanne mine (https://www.jp-petit.org/Presse/Moronvilliers.htm).

13. **Hidden archives and technical issues.** Open Apache directory listings expose an Egyptology folder (http://www.jp-petit.org/papers/egyptology/?C=N;O=A), which is unexpected for a physicist. Several pages also show UTF-8/Latin-1 encoding corruption and an outdated Apache 2.4.7 server.
