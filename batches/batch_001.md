# Analysis of jp-petit.org Page Summaries

All pages come from **jp-petit.org**, the personal site of Jean-Pierre Petit. He is a former CNRS researcher in plasma physics and MHD, a science popularizer, and a comic author. He promotes the non-mainstream "Janus" cosmological model and is also known for writing on UFOs, Ummo and conspiracy topics. Several URLs appear twice (http/https, or with different sort parameters), and duplicates are grouped together below.

## 3. Most Interesting and Noteworthy Pages

1. **The 1988 variable-speed-of-light paper**
   - http://www.jp-petit.org/science/f300/f31a/f31a1.htm and https://www.jp-petit.org/science/f300/f31c/f31c1.htm
   - Petit published a cosmology with jointly varying c, h and G in *Modern Physics Letters A* in 1988–89. This predates the better-known VSL proposals by Moffat (1992) and Albrecht–Magueijo (1999), making it a peer-reviewed work that arguably anticipated an idea later taken up in the mainstream.

2. **Werner Boy's "disappearance"**
   - https://www.jp-petit.org/maths_f/obj_geom/boy.html (and its duplicates)
   - The page tells a romantic tale in which Hilbert's student vanished after discovering the surface. Historically, Boy became a schoolteacher and died in WWI in 1914. It is a nice example of a myth embedded in popular maths.

3. **The lactoèdre**
   - http://www.jp-petit.org/maths_f/obj_geom/lactoedre.html
   - A "surface" supposedly described by "Grossman" in 1956 and conjectured by Hilbert, with renderings by "Iéronimus Holstein and Miroslav Milka." The dairy-themed name and implausible credits strongly suggest a deliberate spoof. That fits Petit's admitted hoaxes (see item 4).

4. **The fake Picasso confession**
   - https://www.jp-petit.org/Divers/Faux/faux_Picasso.htm (see also faux-Daumier.htm)
   - Petit admits that a "1947 Picasso" containing a microcomputer was his own pastiche. He uses it as a lesson in gullibility.

5. **The crosscap cube and 400 Lacanian psychoanalysts**
   - http://www.jp-petit.org/science/maths_f/obj_geom/crosscapcube.html
   - In 1985 a topology model was passed around a psychoanalysis congress, a colourful point where mathematics met Lacanian theory, which borrowed heavily from topology.

6. **The sphere eversion series**
   - https://www.jp-petit.org/maths_f/Retournement_sphere/Retournement_sphere4.htm, etc.
   - A first-hand account of Bernard Morin, the blind mathematician who devised a sphere eversion, together with Petit's own torus eversion work and his 1981 *Comptes Rendus* note with Souriau. These are among the most solid scientific pages on the site.

7. **The Boy-cube page and the Apéry equation**
   - https://www.jp-petit.org/science/maths_f/obj_geom/boycube.html
   - Petit claims that his elliptic-meridian description enabled Apéry's first implicit equation of the Boy surface. This is a genuine link between his work and a known mathematical result.

8. **The Sakharov paper archive and speech**
   - http://www.jp-petit.org/papers/cosmo/Sakharov/ and https://www.jp-petit.org/OVNIS/sakharov_discours.htm
   - Sakharov's 1960s–80s papers on twin and time-reversed universes are the intellectual root of the Janus model. Oddly, the speech is filed in the UFO section.

9. **AI-generated fables**
   - http://www.jp-petit.org/AVANT_DE_VOUS_QUITTER/nouvelles/…/nouvelles_crees_par_les_IA-et_les_internautes.html
   - Very recent material (Nov–Dec 2025) produced by ChatGPT, with one piece attributed to Claude. It shows a long-running personal site from 2002 still evolving.

10. **Directory listings dated to 2026**
    - http://www.jp-petit.org/papers/cosmo/?C=M;O=A
    - The open Apache index exposes an actively maintained research archive, along with an outdated server version (Apache 2.4.7 on Ubuntu).

11. **The Gardanne coal-mine transcripts**
    - https://www.jp-petit.org/Gardanne/rapport01.htm (and rapport05, 10, 12)
    - Rare primary-source labour documents (CHSCT records from 2003) hosted on a physicist's site.

12. **USGS nuclear-test decoupling report**
    - http://www.jp-petit.org/Divers/Nucleaire_souterrain/Rapport_Am%E9ricain.htm
    - A French summary of a real technical report on muffling underground nuclear tests in cavities, which is unusual material for a personal site.

13. **Scepticism and credulity side by side**
    - Paranormal series: https://www.jp-petit.org/Divers/Paranormal/Paranormal2.htm
    - UFO wreck: http://www.jp-petit.org/OVNIS/recuperation_sur_epave.htm
    - The same author carefully debunks "paranormal" tricks as sleight of hand, yet takes an alleged alien-autopsy video at face value and analyses the "six-fingered control system." This contradiction is characteristic of the site.

14. **A Holocaust Remembrance Day page**
    - https://www.jp-petit.org/Israel_Palestine/un_genocide.htm
    - Biblical Joshua 8 is reframed as a "Shoah," and the page was deliberately dated 27 January. It stands out as the most provocative and troubling political page in the batch.

15. **Souriau's contrepet**
    - https://www.jp-petit.org/science/gal_port/contrepet_Souriau.htm
    - A symplectic geometer's spoonerism whose answer is "particule élémentaire," a charming glimpse of French mathematicians' humour.
