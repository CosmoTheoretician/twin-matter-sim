# Analysis of jp-petit.org Page Summaries

All pages are on **jp-petit.org**, the personal site of Jean-Pierre Petit, a French former CNRS astrophysicist and plasma physicist. The site is also a science popularizer's, comic author's and painter's site. To keep lists readable, URLs are shown as paths relative to `https://www.jp-petit.org`. Where the same page appears under both http and https, or under different sort parameters, it is listed once and marked "(dup)".

A few summaries contained no usable content because the page text was empty or the summary was cut off. These are flagged as **[no content]** where relevant: `/science/index.html` (dup), `/VIDEOS_plongees_Google_Earth/index.html` (dup), `/paypalSSF/paypalSSF.htm`, `/Extensions/presentation/pq3.htm`, `/science/ingeph_f/texte_fr/f119.htm`, `/science/divers_f/delires.htm`, `/Site_Anglais/Humor/humor1.htm`.

## 3. Most Interesting and Noteworthy Pages

1. **The CNRS rebuke letter.** `/science/chronique_science/section14.htm`
   A primary document dated 15 March 2000 in which CNRS Section 14 tells Petit that rejecting peer review leads to marginalization and warns him against a "persecution" attitude. It is rare to see an institution's assessment of a researcher published by the researcher himself.

2. **A hostile portrait of a journal founder.** `/science/gal_port/lequeux.htm`
   The page attacks James Lequeux, a founder of *Astronomy & Astrophysics*. It quotes his letter stating that referees are only advisory and the editor alone decides. This is a pointed look at how journal editors hold power over publication.

3. **Real contributions to topology.**
   - `/science/divers_f/cube.htm` and `/science/maths_f/Retournement_sphere/Retournement_sphere0.htm`: Petit published the first readable drawings of sphere eversion in *Pour la Science* in 1979. He poses cube eversion as an open problem and claims a non-trivial torus eversion dating from 1961.
   - `/maths_f/obj_geom/boycube.html`: he claims his description of the Boy surface using elliptic meridians enabled Apéry's implicit equation for it.

   These pages show the mathematically credible side of an author better known for fringe claims.

4. **Psychoanalysis meets topology.** `/maths_f/obj_geom/crosscap.html`
   The page notes that Jacques Lacan placed the "objet a" on the crosscap's central singular point.

5. **A tongue-in-cheek biography.** `/maths_f/obj_geom/withney.html`
   It presents Hassler Whitney as a circus tightrope walker who died falling into the crowd. This is invented; it is not historical fact. Similarly, `/science/gal_port/Lecomte.htm` is an entirely fictional scientist portrait. Both are easy to mistake for genuine biography.

6. **The UFO advocate who debunks the paranormal.** `/Divers/Paranormal/Paranormal3.htm` and `Paranormal6.htm`
   These pages expose conjuring tricks such as a coin secretly swapped for a glass disc. They sit alongside Petit's sympathetic Ummo and UFO pages (`/OVNIS/emission_Ummo.htm`, `/OVNIS/Top_Secret_oct_04.htm`), a striking contrast.

7. **Recent cosmology papers and the Sakharov link.**
   - `/papers/cosmo/HAL2/` lists 2024 papers on "Plugstars" (an alternative to black holes) and the Janus model.
   - `/papers/cosmo/Sakharov/` and `/science/Z-machine/Sakharov_MHD/Sakharov_MHD0.htm` show Petit presenting his work as continuing Andrei Sakharov's ideas.

8. **A comic invention turned patent.** `/Presse/aspirisouffle.htm`
   A device first drawn in Petit's 1979 comic *Si on volait ?* was later patented. Petit adds that a similar device had appeared in an industry magazine.

9. **Unexpected primary sources.**
   - `/Gardanne/rapport06.htm`, `rapport14.htm` and `rapport15.htm` transcribe coal-mine safety-committee minutes.
   - `/Presse/ARMES/drones_controles/audition_Assemblee.htm` is the agenda of a post-Fukushima parliamentary hearing on nuclear policy.

   Both are off-topic for an astrophysics site but have real documentary value.

10. **Science and the military.** `/Geopolitique/Enfants_du_Diable.htm` alongside `/Geopolitique/Discours_adieu_Einsenhower_complet.htm`
    Petit's thesis that military funding has captured physics is paired with Eisenhower's warning about the military-industrial complex. Together they explain much of the site's worldview.

11. **Breadth of the site.** The same domain mixes:
    - peer-reviewed-style physics,
    - 9/11 and "New World Order" conspiracy content (`/Geopolitique/attentats_11sept.htm`, `/Geopolitique/Nouvel_ordre_mondial.htm`, `/BIG BROTHER/signe_main_bush.htm`),
    - chanson lyrics, including a satirical song about researchers (`/AVANT_DE_VOUS_QUITTER/Chansons/Science.htm`),
    - paintings,
    - a donation page that offers to finish a guitar course in return for donations (`/FAIRE_UN_DON/faire_un_don.html`).

12. **Site statistics.** `/Presse/parasitisme_commercial.htm`
    Petit reports about 4,500 visits and about 200 emails a day, and says he spends about 10 hours a day running the site. This is a useful measure of how far the site reached in the mid-2000s.
